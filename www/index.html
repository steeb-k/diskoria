What it does

Everything your drives need

Four test modes cover the full spectrum of storage health visibility — from safe read-only scans to thorough destructive verification.

Sector Test

Sequentially reads every sector of a physical disk in 1 MiB aligned chunks. Reports good, slow (≥200 ms), and bad sectors with a real-time heatmap grid — without touching your data.

Read-only · Non-destructive

Speed Test

File-based benchmarks on any volume: sequential read & write plus random 4 KiB I/O. Workload profiles are automatically tuned for NVMe, SATA, USB, and removable media.

Temp file only

Destructive Test

Writes a deterministic pattern to every sector, then reads it back to verify. Locks and dismounts all volumes before writing. Use to fully certify or sanitize a disk you intend to wipe.

Erases all data

SMART Health

Surfaces WMI-backed SMART health hints alongside each sector scan — status, partition layout, BitLocker awareness, and drive metadata visible at a glance.

WMI · Read-only

Reading the Sector Map

Each of the 1000 cells represents a contiguous span of the disk's capacity in address order. Color encodes both correctness and speed in a single glance.

Not scanned — pending or not yet started
Good (heat) — darker = slower, brighter = faster
Slow — average read ≥ 200 ms
Bad / Error — at least one read failure
